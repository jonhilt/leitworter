# Twenty variations

**What it is:**

Design it twice, taken to a bigger number. The agent generates many options at once and builds a small tool for comparing them, instead of you iterating one version at a time.

**When / why:**

You're choosing a UI layout, some copy, a name or a design, and you'd otherwise go back and forth with the agent, one tweak per round. Ask for twenty variations and a little page to combine and test them, so you can compare them all in one place and pick. For example: "Generate 20 variations of this pricing card and a little page that lets me combine and test them."

**Exact prompt:**

> Generate 20 variations and a little page that lets me combine and test them.

**Why this packs more than a tip:**

The large number widens the search across possible solutions, well past the obvious two or three. The "little page" turns comparing them into a tool instead of a slow back-and-forth.
