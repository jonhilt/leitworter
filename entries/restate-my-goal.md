# Restate my goal

**What it is:**

Teach-back. The agent has to say back what it thinks you're trying to achieve before it acts, so any misunderstanding comes out up front.

**When / why:**

Use it before a big change or a long autonomous run. A wrong reading of the goal costs hours, but you can catch it in one line if the agent states it first.

**Exact prompt:**

> restate in your own words what you think my goal is

**Why this packs more than a tip:**

"In your own words" stops the agent echoing your prompt back. "Goal" asks for the outcome you want, not the list of tasks, so gaps between what you said and what you meant show up.
