# Contributing

## What this is

An editorial archive of **Leitwörter** — short, concept-anchored prompts — not a community pastebin of AI tips.

## What to submit

One pull request = one entry (or a small batch of clearly separate entries).

Copy `entries/_TEMPLATE.md` to `entries/<slug>.md` and fill every required field.

### Required

- Exact example prompt (blockquoted, copy-paste ready)
- Why denser than a tip (1–2 lines)
- Source: person, date, URL

### Strongly preferred

- A short gloss from the source (their words, attributed)
- Where it pairs with another Leitwort (e.g. brittle/durable quotes pokayoke)

### Rejected by default

- Paraphrased “improved” prompts without the original wording
- Anonymous tips / Discord lore without a public URL
- Long SOPs disguised as one Leitwort
- Engagement-bait “prompt packs”
- Anything that only works as a tip, not as a named concept or forced comparison

## Review

Maintainers merge. Passing the shape bar is necessary but not sufficient — we prefer practitioners over growth hacks. Disagreement is fine; please keep tone civil.
