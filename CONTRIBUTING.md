# Contributing

## What this is

An editorial archive of Leitwörter: short, concept-anchored prompts. Not a pastebin of AI tips.

## What to submit

One pull request covers one entry (or a small batch of clearly separate entries).

Copy `entries/_TEMPLATE.md` to `entries/<slug>.md` and fill every required field.

### Required

- What it is: one plain sentence naming the concept
- When / why: one concrete scenario. When would you reach for this leading word with an agent? Naming the word is not enough. The entry has to answer the use case without sending the reader to a book
- Exact example prompt (blockquoted, copy-paste ready). The leading word alone is fine if When / why carries the use case
- One or two lines on why this packs more than a tip
- A source. If you quote someone, give their name, the date, and a URL. If it comes from a book, name the book and the public post that treats the phrase as a Leitwort. If it is first-party or from the community, give a name (or handle), a date, and a short note on how you used it. You do not need an earlier public post. Private community shares are fine (for example in Skool); a maintainer can file the entry. Once merged, the PR or commit is the public record. Mark `Origin: first-party` or `Origin: community` on the entry. We will not merge an anonymous "works for me" with no name and no use note.

### Strongly preferred

- A short gloss from the source (their words, attributed)
- Links to related entries

### Rejected by default

- Paraphrased "improved" prompts without the original wording
- Anonymous tips with no name and no use note
- Long SOPs dressed up as one Leitwort
- Engagement-bait "prompt packs"
- Tips that only work as advice, not as a named concept or forced comparison
- Entries that name the word but leave when and why to the book
- Long quotes from books. Name the concept and the book. Point at a public post that uses it as a Leitwort.

## First-party and community finds

You do not need a tweet or blog post first. If a leading word worked for you, submit it.

Same shape bar as everything else. The only difference is the source line: you, a date, and a concrete note on how you used it. Private shares from the community are fine; a maintainer turns them into an entry.

Do not lower the bar to make room for tip dumps. A named concept or forced comparison is still required. What it is and When / why are still required.

## Review

Maintainers merge. Passing the shape bar is necessary but not enough. We prefer practitioners over growth hacks. Keep the tone civil.
