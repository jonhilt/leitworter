# Contributing

## What this is

An editorial archive of Leitwörter: short, concept-anchored prompts. Not a pastebin of AI tips.

## What to submit

One pull request = one entry (or a small batch of clearly separate entries).

Copy `entries/_TEMPLATE.md` to `entries/<slug>.md` and fill every required field.

### Required

- Exact example prompt (blockquoted, copy-paste ready)
- Why denser than a tip (1–2 lines)
- Source (one of):
  - **Quoted:** person, date, public URL (X, blog, etc.), or book + the public post that treats the phrase as a Leitwort
  - **First-party / community:** your name (or handle), date, and one short line of lived use (where you used it, what got better). No prior public post required. Community members can share privately (e.g. in Skool); a maintainer can file the entry. Once merged, the PR (or commit) is the public record. Mark `Origin: first-party` or `Origin: community` on the entry.

### Strongly preferred

- A short gloss from the source (their words, attributed)
- Links to related entries

### Rejected by default

- Paraphrased "improved" prompts without the original wording
- Anonymous tips with no name and no lived-use note
- Long SOPs dressed up as one Leitwort
- Engagement-bait "prompt packs"
- Tips that only work as advice, not as a named concept or forced comparison
- Long quotes from books. Name the concept and the book. Point at a public post that uses it as a Leitwort.

## First-party and community finds

You do not need a tweet or blog post first. If a leading word worked for you, submit it.

Same shape bar as everything else. The difference is only the source line: you, a date, and a concrete lived-use note. Private shares from the community are fine; a maintainer turns them into an entry.

Do not lower the bar to make room for tip dumps. Named concept (or forced comparison) still required.

## Review

Maintainers merge. Passing the shape bar is necessary but not enough. We prefer practitioners over growth hacks. Keep the tone civil.
